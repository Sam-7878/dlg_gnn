## 2. Scientific-Review Assistance & Elimination of False Denials

In earlier rounds, the attestation matrix contained a rigid categorical denial:
> *"No AI model generated ... scientific interpretations."*

As documented in the P3 Work Order and background discussion, AI-assisted tools were actively used not merely for grammar or LaTeX typesetting, but for **critical peer review, manuscript framing critique, software-development suggestions, and cross-document discrepancy checking**. 

To maintain strict truthfulness:
1. The categorical denial was deleted.
2. The exact operational boundary was formalized:
   > *"AI-assisted tools were used to provide drafting, draft organization, software-development and code-review assistance, cross-document consistency checking, and scientific-review feedback. All numerical analyses, statistical outputs, source verifications, interpretation choices, and final scientific conclusions were independently checked and approved by the authors. No AI tool acted as an autonomous scientific authority."*
3. **Automated Test:** `tests/benchmark/publication_p3/test_no_false_no_scientific_interpretation_claim.py` (PASS)
