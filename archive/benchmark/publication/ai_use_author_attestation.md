## 3. Scope and Boundary Declarations

1. **AI Suggestion vs. Scientific Authority**:
   AI-assisted tools were used to provide drafting, draft organization, software-development and code-review assistance, cross-document consistency checking, and scientific-review feedback. All numerical analyses, statistical outputs, source verifications, interpretation choices, and final scientific conclusions were independently checked and approved by the authors. No AI tool acted as an autonomous scientific authority.

2. **Integrity of Frozen Benchmark Artifacts**:
   No AI tool generated, altered, or imputed the frozen Round 5 raw benchmark data (`benchmark_raw.csv`, SHA-256: `39a497efe81a0d2630d8817e653d35b01bbb141de4a8d008a46a8c13f1c8375c`) or the model-dataset support matrix (`c58dbca9a9e1ed14dfc025075820a3ad745f6cb70be77764c265d90af3522914`). While AI tools read frozen tables to summarize findings, all reported numbers strictly reflect authentic execution outputs.

3. **Software & Test Suite Verification**:
   All scripts, execution backends, and test suites developed with AI assistance were subjected to automated regression testing (including strict numerical equivalence tests against dense reference implementations) and line-by-line manual code inspection prior to integration.
