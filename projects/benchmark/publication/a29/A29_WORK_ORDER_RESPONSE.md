Two caveats about the hardware. First, under WSL a GPU job that exceeds the 24 GiB card spills into shared system memory. Runs marked "spilled" are slower, and their timings are not comparable with in-VRAM runs. Second, two spilling jobs at once caused out-of-memory failures. Those first attempts are kept as `attempt1_*` (or `attempt2_*`) and disclosed, and the cells were rerun one at a time. The Reddit-Syn GADNR retry, which exhausted host RAM, was repeated inside a systemd memory cap so that only that process could fail.
