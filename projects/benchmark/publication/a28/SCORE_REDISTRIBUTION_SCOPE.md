# A28 score redistribution scope

Provider terms checked on 6 November 2026 KST. This review concerns the retained node-level files and their linkage to labels, node IDs, masks, or a documented node order. The proposed submission policy keeps those files internal for every dataset. The corresponding author must confirm that scope and both authors must approve the resulting submission hashes.

[Kaggle provider metadata](https://www.kaggle.com/api/v1/datasets/view/ellipticco/elliptic-data-set) · [CC legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/legalcode) | Internal only. |

Injected anomaly labels do not remove upstream rights or provenance. The A28 public evidence scope covers report-level statistics, scalar verification, audit source and inputs for the exact rank test, and artifact hashes. Per-node files remain local. The preprint license applies to the authors’ manuscript. An author decision that broadens or changes the access statement requires a fresh approval of the final files.
