# Full-archive observed span is a retrospective oracle

`L = last_valid_transfer - first_valid_transfer` uses the complete archive. It cannot generally be known at the original cutoff before completion, and it is certainly unavailable at first transfer plus seven days when later transfers exist. `oracle_lifetime_baselines.csv` marks every row `ORACLE/RETROSPECTIVE DIAGNOSTIC ONLY`, `operational_feature=false`, and `performance_upper_bound=false`. Its score orientation, `-log1p(L in days)`, was fixed before test evaluation. No future span is used by any fitted R319 cutoff-admissible baseline or neural arm.

On the distinct 7-day test, the full-archive span score AP is 0.9464 and ROC-AUC 0.9162, compared with 56.16% positive prevalence. A *cutoff-time* retained-node-count logistic baseline has AP 0.8943; the four-count baseline AP is 0.8595. These metrics diagnose a strong size/activity signal but do not identify why the source labels and activity differ.

Of 3,627 seven-day test contracts, 1,162 have observed span under one day, including 1,147 positives (98.71%). The 1,929 contracts with at least seven days of recorded activity include 379 positives (19.65%). In that **future-conditioned** subset, preserved full-summary MLP AP is 0.5834 and preserved GIN AP 0.2827. Its AP baseline and population differ from the full test; these figures are not a paired causal effect of the conditioning and must not be sold as deployment performance. An archive's last transaction is not an observed contract death or a known fraud-discovery time. Confidence intervals and low-support exceptions are recorded in `lifetime_shortcut_audit.csv` and `subgroup_support.csv`.
