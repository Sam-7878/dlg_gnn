\section{Data Semantics, Temporal Composition and Entities}
One observation is a token/contract graph at its maximum valid transaction timestamp. The label is inherited from the upstream contract Category, with Category 0 mapped to the positive class. The benchmark is not a collection of individually labeled transactions, and label discovery timestamps are unavailable. Full-history degree features are computed before capping the message-passing topology.

The split-composition, entity-overlap, and timestamp-tie CSV files report all split and six-slice chain supports. Chain-qualified contracts are disjoint across splits. Cross-chain hexadecimal-address overlap is reported separately; same hexadecimal strings are not assumed to represent one contract. Sender/receiver identities are not contained in the packed payload, so this evidence does not establish wallet independence.

\section{Objective and Summary Controls}
\input{../data/table_controls.tex}
The weighted BCE objective is
\[
 -\frac{1}{N}\sum_i \left[w_+ y_i\log\sigma(z_i)+(1-y_i)\log(1-\sigma(z_i))\right],
 \qquad w_+=N_{-,tr}/N_{+,tr}.
\]
The weighted focal variant multiplies each sample term by $(1-p_t)^2$. It is not an effective-number class-balanced objective. \texttt{loss\_fairness\_results.csv} provides the matched 2$\times$2 objective matrix; \texttt{simple\_baselines.csv} and \texttt{chain\_ablation.csv} provide the other controls. All new neural conditions have a ceiling of 60 epochs with patience five; they are kept separate from the historical 20-epoch panel.

Summary features comprise node and edge counts, directed density $m/[n(n-1)]$, and the mean, median and maximum of retained in-, out-, and total-degree. They also include the same three summaries of each full-history log-degree node feature and chain one-hot. Degrees count repeated edges. The median averages the middle pair when necessary. \texttt{summary\_scaler.json} records training-only means and scales. LR uses balanced sample weights, $C=1$ and a 2,000-iteration limit. The MLP uses width 48, ReLU and dropout 0.3. The no-chain GIN removes the chain inputs from the classifier head.

\section{Statistical Audit}
\input{../data/table_statistics.tex}
The corrected implementation groups equal-score thresholds and agrees with scikit-learn AP, including on bootstrap samples with duplicated observations. \texttt{ap\_tie\_audit.csv} supplies explicit original-versus-corrected examples. The point estimate is a mean of seed-matched AP differences. A shared resampled sample index is used within each comparison; randomization swaps score vectors within each seed. Its null is not invariant to monotone transformation of just one method's score scale.

The two-sided simulated randomization correction is $(1+n_{\rm extreme})/(10000+1)$. The Holm family contains exactly the original seven comparisons. Any additional control contrasts are marked exploratory and are not silently added to that family. No inference here accounts for all possible temporal or graph-induced dependence, and seeds are not treated as additional independently sampled test contracts.

\section{Strict-Time and Operational Outputs}
\texttt{strict\_time\_source\_audit.csv} checks all 3,648 held-out source files against both packed tensors, including the pre-cap history features. Strict-time inputs remove cutoff-time rows from both graph and degree features. Twenty fixed checkpoints, five per original architecture, are evaluated without test-driven refitting. \texttt{strict\_time\_sensitivity.csv} reports AP, ROC-AUC, F1, Brier, ECE and NLL alongside AP differences. These tests do not establish long-term temporal transportability.

\texttt{operational\_metrics.csv} contains per-seed Precision/Recall at top 1\% (37 alerts), top 5\% (183 alerts), and fraud captures in the first 50 and 100 alerts. Equal-score ordering uses the frozen input order; no threshold is tuned using test labels. Ensemble metrics are one aggregate each. \texttt{reliability\_bin\_support.csv} contains all calibration-bin counts, mean positive probabilities, and empirical positive fractions. \texttt{calibration\_direction\_audit.csv} summarizes the bin-level directions.
