# Statistical and calibration audit

The seven original comparisons were recomputed from the same frozen test identities and score vectors, using 10,000 ordinary paired bootstrap draws, 10,000 class-stratified draws and 10,000 score-swap randomizations per comparison. Seeds and resampling order match the historical finalizer (`20260910 + comparison index`). The new values are in `supplement/statistical_comparisons_corrected.csv`, with raw and Holm-adjusted p-values in the same file.

An actual historical implementation issue was found: fast AP used row-level precision at every positive after a stable score sort. This agrees with standard AP when scores are distinct, but not when scores are tied. Bootstrap duplication creates ties even when the original scores are distinct. Corrected AP groups equal-score thresholds and is checked against `sklearn.metrics.average_precision_score`, including constant scores and duplicated bootstrap samples. Point estimates are unchanged in the seven comparisons; confidence intervals change modestly. Frozen original artifacts are preserved. `ap_tie_audit.csv` records numerical counterexamples.

The finite-randomization correction is `(extreme + 1)/(R + 1)`. At R=10,000 the minimum is 1/10001 = 0.0000999900009999, displayed as 0.0001. These are simulated randomization probabilities, not exact enumeration probabilities. Five of seven comparisons have this raw minimum; their seven-comparison Holm p is 0.00069993. MC vs temperature scaling has raw p=0.0459954 and Holm p=0.0919908. The fixed MC ensemble vs deep ensemble has raw/Holm p=0.1278872. Consequently neither of the latter two comparisons is called familywise significant at 0.05.

All MC comparisons reuse the same five frozen MC vectors. Event IDs, timestamps and labels are matched exactly. Temperature scaling preserves each seed's observed ranking, but score-swap randomization mixes score magnitudes across methods, so its null need not be preserved by transforming just one method. The difference between raw p=0.0001 and 0.0460 is not a vector-cache bug. It remains after correcting the bootstrap AP implementation.

Inference is conditional on the trained checkpoints; test sample indices are resampled and seeds are not. Sample-index resampling assumes exchangeability that residual temporal or shared-actor dependence can violate. These are conditional sensitivity analyses, not proof of independent future-deployment performance.

The six supplemental control contrasts form a separate, explicitly exploratory family, with its own Holm sensitivity in `control_statistical_comparisons.csv`. They are not included retroactively in the primary seven-comparison family or described as pre-specified.

Temperature parameters are 0.4921, 0.4344, 0.5354, 0.4322 and 0.2969. The transform sharpens logits: below-0.5 probabilities shrink and above-0.5 probabilities grow. Reliability bins use positive probability, not maximum predicted-class confidence. Many low-probability bins overestimate positive frequency while some high-score bins underestimate it. The manuscript uses mixed miscalibration and explains the direction explicitly. Full bin counts and directions are supplied in `supplement/reliability_bin_support.csv` and `data/calibration_direction_audit.csv`.

Operational budgets are frozen at ceil(1%×3648)=37 and ceil(5%×3648)=183, plus 50 and 100 alerts. Stable input order resolves score ties. No threshold or budget is selected by test performance.
