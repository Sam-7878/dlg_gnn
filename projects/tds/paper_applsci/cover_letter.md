Please consider the manuscript “A Validity-First Evaluation of Graph Neural Networks under Temporal Distribution Shift” for the Applied Sciences Special Issue “Graph Neural Networks: Theory, Methods and Applications.”

The contribution is an auditable evaluation methodology for dynamic financial graphs. It fixes prediction-time information admissibility and separates rare-event ranking from probability calibration, using a reproducible multi-chain contract-graph benchmark. The paper evaluates reference GNN encoders, simple structural-summary controls, matched loss objectives and validation-only calibration. Its conclusions are specific to the evaluated information contract and implementations.

The work fits the Special Issue's dynamic/temporal GNN methodology and financial-modeling topics. Source identity, sample and label semantics, timestamp sensitivity, per-seed predictions, statistical implementation corrections and editable figures are documented. The terminal test horizon and retrospective label limitations are stated explicitly.
